PRÉCIS

The essence of any page.

Précis is a browser extension that summarizes the article, report or post you are reading in clear bullet points, then answers your follow-up questions about it, right in the side panel.

Add to Chrome

Works in Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers such as Brave and Edge. Requires a Google account.

What it does

  • One-click summaries in concise bullet points, streamed in as they are written.
  • Follow-up questions answered from the page you are reading.
  • Think deeper for harder questions: Précis reasons before it answers.
  • One chat per tab that stays with the page, and notices when the page changes.
  • Private by design: no ads, no tracking, and the pages you read are never stored on our servers. See the privacy policy.
