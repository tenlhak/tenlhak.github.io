PRÉCIS

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Précis is a Chrome extension that summarizes the web page you are reading and answers your follow-up questions about it. This policy explains what data Précis handles, why, and where it goes.

  • Précis reads and sends page content only in tabs where you have opened Précis, to produce the page description, summary or answers.
  • Précis does not store the pages you read, your questions or the answers on its servers.
  • Précis does not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

What Précis handles

  • Page content. When you open the Précis panel on a page, Précis reads that page and sends its address, title and the beginning of its text (up to about 8,000 characters) to write a one-line description of the page. When you ask for a summary or ask a question, Précis sends the page's address, title and text, along with your question and the earlier messages of that chat. Précis can only read what your browser displays; it does not get around paywalls or log in anywhere on your behalf.
  • Your Google account email. You sign in with the Google account signed into Chrome. Précis receives your email address and a temporary sign-in token from Google; the backend verifies that token with Google (remembering a successful check for up to 5 minutes). Précis does not receive your password.
  • Basic request logs. For each request, the backend records the date and time, which feature was used, a random chat ID, your email address, the length of the page text, how long the response took, the amount of AI processing used, and the type of any error. These logs never contain page text, your questions or the answers.

How it is used

This information is used only to provide Précis's features — the page description, summary and answers — to keep the service secure (making sure requests come from a signed-in Google account), and to diagnose problems. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

Who processes it

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) hosts the Précis backend in the United States (us-east-1) and stores the basic request logs.
  • Moonshot AI, the provider of the Kimi AI model, receives the page content and questions described above in order to generate descriptions, summaries and answers. Its handling of that data is governed by Moonshot AI's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google provides sign-in and confirms that your sign-in token is valid.

Précis does not share or sell your data to anyone else.

How long it is kept

  • On Précis's servers: page content, questions and answers are processed to produce a response and then discarded; they are not saved. Basic request logs are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • In your browser: your current chat (including the page text it is about) is kept in Chrome's temporary session storage so it survives closing and reopening the panel. It is deleted when you close the tab, after 30 minutes without activity, when you start a new chat, or when you quit Chrome.
  • At Moonshot AI: retention of data sent for processing is governed by Moonshot AI's policies.

Permissions

Précis asks Chrome for access to the sites you visit so that it can read the page you are on when you use it. It reads pages only in tabs where you have opened the Précis panel: when the panel opens, when you ask for a summary or ask a question, and when that tab's page changes, so the description and chat stay in sync with the page. Other permissions are used to keep one chat per tab, notice when a page changes or reloads, expire idle chats, and sign you in with Google.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Use New chat or close the tab to clear a chat from your browser at any time.
  • Use Sign out in the panel to sign out and revoke Précis's access to your Google sign-in.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all of its data from your browser.
  • To ask about or request deletion of the basic logs associated with your email address before they expire, contact us at the address below.

Children

Précis is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
